The morning train was late again, so
Daniel walked the long way to the
office along the river. He liked the
walk more than he admitted to anyone.
The water was calm, and a few early
rowers were already gliding past the
old stone bridge.

By the time he reached his desk, the
first meeting had started without him.
Nobody seemed to mind. His manager
nodded from across the room and
pointed at an empty chair near the
window. Daniel sat down quietly and
opened his notebook.

Later that afternoon, the rain began.
It fell steadily against the glass
until the whole city looked soft and
gray. He watched it for a while, and
then he went back to work.
